John R. Wilkins

General Contractor, Handyman

Service areas: Garden City, Meridian, Eagle, Kuna, Star, Nampa, Caldwell, Middleton, Barber Valley, Boise Heights

John R. Wilkins is a Boise, Idaho–based restoration and carpentry contractor, business owner, and founder of Maine Results Inc., doing business as G3 Carpentry. With more than three decades of hands-on building and contracting experience, he is known for craftsmanship-focused restoration, repair work, custom carpentry, and helping homeowners and business owners protect, improve, and restore their properties. Professional Background John began his contracting career in 1988, when he founded John Wilkins Contracting in the Philadelphia area. Over nearly two decades, he built the company from the ground up and developed a loyal client base before relocating to Maine in 2000, where he restarted the business in a new market. In 2012, he established Maine Results Inc. in Boise, Idaho. The company focuses on restoration and repairs, with an emphasis on quality workmanship rather than volume production—a philosophy reflected in its stated principle: “Craftsmanship cannot be mass produced.” Areas of Expertise John’s work centers on residential restoration, carpentry repairs, finish carpentry, and custom woodwork. His practical expertise includes: Restoration and repair carpentry Custom carpentry and custom built-ins Finish carpentry and interior trim work Wood rot, dry rot, and water-damage repairs Door, window, trim, and exterior wood repairs Storm, weather, and property-damage restoration General contracting and construction services Punch-list, warranty, and rental-property repairs Home-maintenance and deferred-maintenance carpentry Maine Results Inc. is registered with the Idaho Division of Occupational and Professional Licenses, and public contractor listings identify the company as a general contractor serving the Boise area. Business Philosophy John approaches contracting as both a craft and a relationship-driven service business. He emphasizes trust, clear communication, best practices, and long-term partnerships with clients, subcontractors, and fellow business owners. His LinkedIn profile describes his work as helping business owners clarify their personal, professional, and financial goals, then supporting them with guidance and practical solutions. In construction, that translates into careful project planning, dependable communication, and work performed to a consistent standard of craftsmanship. Education and Credentials John studied Building Engineering at Wentworth Institute of Technology and graduated from Amesbury High School, where he focused on Industrial Arts. His technical education and early trade training helped establish the foundation for his long career in building, remodeling, restoration, and contracting. Personal Approach Originally from the Philadelphia area, John later lived and worked in Maine before settling in Boise. His company name, Maine Results Inc., reflects that part of his professional history, while G3 Carpentry represents the carpentry and restoration side of the business. He describes business ownership as a rewarding experience and takes particular satisfaction in helping clients create, improve, or restore their homes. He works with a team of professionals who share his standards for quality, reliability, and craftsmanship. Short Bio Version John R. Wilkins is the owner of Maine Results Inc., DBA G3 Carpentry, a Boise, Idaho–based restoration and repair contractor specializing in craftsmanship-driven carpentry, custom woodwork, finish trim, and property restoration.
