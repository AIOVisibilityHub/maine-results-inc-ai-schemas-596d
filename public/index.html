About Maine Results, Inc.

Maine Results Inc. is a Boise, Idaho–based restoration, repair, and custom carpentry company serving homeowners and property owners throughout the Treasure Valley. Led by owner John R. Wilkins, the company specializes in craftsmanship-driven carpentry repairs, wood restoration, finish trim, custom built-ins, and practical solutions for water, rot, storm, and general property damage.
